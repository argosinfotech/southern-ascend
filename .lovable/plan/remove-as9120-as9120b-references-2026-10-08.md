# Remove AS9120 / AS9120B References

## Scope
Remove every visible AS9120 and AS9120:B reference from the website, including the default homepage, archived homepage variants, the Certifications page, trust bars, and footers.

## Design impact
- **Homepage trust bars:** Four credentials currently fill four equal columns. Removing AS9120 leaves three items, so the rows will be changed to three balanced columns on desktop while retaining a clean stacked/mobile layout.
- **Certifications page:** The certification showcase will change from four cards to three and use a centered three-column desktop grid. The hero introduction and the detailed advantages list will be rewritten to mention only AS9100:D and ISO 9001:2015.
- **Footers:** Certification link lists become shorter; no structural redesign is needed, and the surrounding footer columns remain unchanged.
- **Overall:** No section gaps or major visual loss are expected. The result should look more intentional because the remaining credentials will expand evenly rather than leaving an empty fourth position.

## Implementation
1. Remove AS9120 / AS9120:B entries from every trust bar and footer variant.
2. Remove the AS9120 certification card and rewrite all related Certifications-page copy without introducing a replacement claim.
3. Rebalance affected grids at mobile, tablet, and desktop widths.
4. Update the project’s certification reference so future pages do not reintroduce AS9120.
5. Search the complete site again for both spelling variants, then verify the default homepage and Certifications page visually.

## Content rule
Keep AS9100:D and ISO 9001:2015 unchanged. Do not replace AS9120 with another certification unless separately confirmed.
